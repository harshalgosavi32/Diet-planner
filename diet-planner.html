Tips for Healthy Living
Simple habits that make a big difference in your health journey.
💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink 8–10 glasses of water daily. Start your morning with warm water and lemon.

🌙

Don't Skip Dinner

Eat a light dinner at least 2 hours before sleeping to aid digestion and sleep quality.

🥦

Eat More Vegetables

Include at least 2–3 servings of seasonal vegetables in your daily meals.

🚶

Walk Daily

A 30-minute brisk walk after meals boosts metabolism and helps maintain a healthy weight.

🍽️

Eat Mindfully

Chew slowly, avoid screens while eating, and stop when 80% full to prevent overeating.

🧘

Manage Stress

Stress increases cortisol and triggers emotional eating. Practice yoga or deep breathing daily.

😴

Sleep 7–8 Hours

Poor sleep disrupts hunger hormones. Consistent, quality sleep is essential for weight management.

🚫

Reduce Junk Food

Limit processed foods, packaged snacks, and sugary drinks. Replace with whole foods like fruits and nuts.
